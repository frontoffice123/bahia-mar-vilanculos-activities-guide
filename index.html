Activities in Vilanculos and the Bazaruto Archipelago

Vilankulo and the Bazaruto Archipelago are world-famous for many beach holiday activities. Most are centred around the Indian Ocean which laps our shores with island trips, game fishing, scuba diving, kite-boarding and whale watching to name but a few.

How to book: activities are booked directly with the operator, or with the help of our reception once you're here. Some activities require a minimum number of participants.

Trips to the islands

There are several trips to the islands and other interesting spots. These trips can be done by traditional dhow to the closest areas like Magaruque island, Shell island and the South point of Benguerra; or by speedboat to the North point of Benguerra, Bazaruto island, Paradise island and Bangue island. We can advise you and offer you the best recommendations.

A protected archipelago

The islands and surrounding waters make up the Bazaruto Archipelago National Park, a protected marine reserve. A park entry fee applies to every visit. Some operators include it in their rate and others charge it separately on the day, so please confirm what is included when you book.

Drones are not permitted

Flying a drone inside Bazaruto Archipelago National Park is prohibited without formal written authorisation from the Department of Defence and the Civil Aviation Authority of Mozambique, validated by the Park Administration. Unauthorised drones are confiscated and returned only after payment of a substantial fine. Please leave your drone at the hotel unless you hold current permits. For the national rules, see drone-laws.com.

The trips include national park fees, snorkelling equipment, shade, chairs and table on the beach, soft drinks and a fresh fish lunch cooked on the beach. The Bazaruto day snorkels Two Mile Reef — the 'Aquarium' — which has diverse coral and thousands of tropical fish, from tiny clownfish to large wrasses and parrotfish; look for octopus, lionfish and lobster. It also takes in the climb up the Bazaruto dune, with a slow cruise along Benguerra on the way back in the hope of flamingos. Santa Carolina is the only deserted island in the archipelago, with an abandoned 1950s hotel and reefs you can snorkel straight off the shore. A private charter on Mothassane takes up to 14 people to the island or islands of your choice, with catering from $20 per person.

Good to know: Snorkelling is part of both day trips, with equipment provided. Children under 10 get a 50% discount. Departure times move with the tide. Dolphins are often seen on the way out or back.

Magaruque is the closest island to Vilankulo, with easy drift snorkelling off the base reef, beaches to explore and dunes to climb on the eastern side. Bazaruto is further out, about two and a half hours' sailing, with spectacular dunes on the south point; snorkelling on this trip is at Two Mile Reef, tides permitting, and there is drifting off the south point of Bazaruto with the current. Both safaris include a seafood lunch on the beach, snacks on route, refreshments and snorkelling equipment, and exclude park entry fees.

Good to know: Two Mile Reef is reached on the Bazaruto trip when the tides of the day allow. Children under 12 are half price. Departure times move with the tide. Sailaway also run sunset safaris and multi-day island trips — see their site.

Deep sea and game fishing

Full dayPer boatBook ahead

Marlin, sailfish, tuna, wahoo, dorado and GTs, year-round, on fully rigged sport fishers out of Vilankulos.

Duration
Full day, up to 10 hours
Operator
FishBazaruto · Big Blue Vilankulo
Website
fishbazaruto.com · bigbluevilankulo.com
Details
FishBazaruto, 38ft SuperCat — full day, 10hrs, max 6 anglers$1,450

FishBazaruto is run by Captain Duarte Rato, who has fished the archipelago for 25 years. Their 38ft SuperCat is the largest sport fisher working out of Vilankulos, with a 16ft beam and space for six anglers plus three crew. The day rate covers captain and crew, conventional tackle and terminal gear, bait, fuel, ice, fishing licences and park fees, breakfast rolls and fruit, and an onboard barbecue for up to four (then $25 per person). Drinks are excluded, though cooler boxes, water and ice are provided. Heavy tackle marlin seasons run May to June and September to mid-December. Big Blue Vilankulo also run charters out of town — see their site for boats and rates.

Good to know: FishBazaruto: WhatsApp +258 84 639 0466. Reception can put you in touch with either operator.

Seahorse safari

2 hoursGood with kidsTide dependent

A snorkelling tour on a local dhow to the shallow seagrass beds where the bay's seahorses live.

Duration
About 2 hours
Price
On request
Operator
APARCO — local fisheries committee
Website
aparco.org/seahorseprotection
Details

The local fisheries committee leads tours on traditional dhows out to the shallow seagrass habitats where seahorses can be observed. Three of the world's fifty-plus seahorse species are found here; two are listed as vulnerable on the IUCN Redlist. The tours grew out of a 2018 campaign, run with the fisheries authorities and supported by Bahia Mar, to end seahorse poaching in the bay — the income they generate is what makes protection work.

Good to know: Shallow, gentle and suitable for families. Timed to the tide, so departures are early morning or late afternoon.

Visit our school

Bahia Mar's support of community preschools, which began in 2014, has empowered over 400 children to learn Portuguese before they commenced primary school. Bahia Mar has provided professional learn-through-play teacher training to local community volunteers who give their mornings to develop the minds, hearts and hands of local three to five-year-olds. Guests are invited to visit a school, meet the kids and share the experience — just ask at reception. It only takes six adults with big hearts to change the lives of over 100 children. bahiamarclub.com/bonguili

Responsible tourism

Tourism has a big impact on a small community such as Vilankulo. We ask you to bear this in mind, to be a responsible tourist and to respect the local community. Seahorse poaching in the bay was once widespread. Signage along the Vilankulo coast was designed by APARCO, Bahia Mar, African Parks and the local authorities, and the seahorse tours now give the fishing community an alternative income.
